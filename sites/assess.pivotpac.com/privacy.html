Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

You trust us with your family's details, so here is exactly what we collect and what we do with it. This policy covers pivotpac.com, assess.pivotpac.com and parents.pivotpac.com.

What we collect

Only what you give us:

  • Assessments. Name, email, optional phone, sport, school level and your answers and score. The parent assessment also asks for your athlete's first name. High school athletes also give a parent or guardian's email.
  • Contact form. Name, email, whether you're an athlete, parent or institution and your message.
  • Newsletter and free downloads. Your email address.
  • Purchases. Your email and what you bought. Payment is handled by our payment processor. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Booking a call. Calendly collects the details you enter when you book.

Our sites don't use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Calendly sets its own cookies on its booking pages under its own policy.

How we use it

  • To follow up on your assessment results and answer your questions.
  • To deliver what you asked for, like a download, a purchase or a booked session.
  • To send our newsletter if you signed up. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand which resources help families most, so we can improve them.

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We share it only with the services that run this business for us, and only so they can do that job:

  • Formspree, which receives our assessment and form submissions.
  • Calendly, which runs our booking pages.
  • Our payment processor, for purchases.
  • Our email service, to deliver downloads and newsletters.
  • Vercel and Hostinger, which host our websites.

We'll also share information if the law requires it.

Athletes under 18

We work with high school, college and professional athletes and their families. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you're a high school athlete, we ask for a parent or guardian's email and their permission before you start the assessment. We include that parent or guardian in our follow-up.

Parents can ask us at any time to show them or delete what we have about their child. Email connect@pivotpac.com.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while we're working with you or following up, then delete what we no longer need. You can ask us to delete yours sooner.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe from any email with the link at the bottom.
  • Ask to see, correct or delete your information by emailing connect@pivotpac.com. We'll answer within 30 days.

Do Not Track. We don't track you across other websites, so we treat every visitor the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

Keeping it safe

Our sites use encrypted connections, and we limit who can see submissions. No system is perfect, so if something ever goes wrong we'll tell affected families promptly.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Big changes get a clear note on our site.
